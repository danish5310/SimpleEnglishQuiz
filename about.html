About Us 🌎

What is Amazing Animals?

Amazing Animals is a simple educational website created for kindergarten children aged 5 to 6 years old.

Our website helps children learn about animals through colourful learning materials and a fun online quiz.

Our Objective 🎯

  • Learn about different animals.
  • Understand where animals live.
  • Learn what animals eat.
  • Have fun while learning.
  • Test knowledge through an online quiz.
