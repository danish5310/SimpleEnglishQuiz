Meet the Animals! 🐾

Let's learn about some amazing animals!

🦁 Lion

Lions are big cats. They live in groups called prides.

Food: Meat 🥩

🐘 Elephant

Elephants are very large animals with long trunks.

Food: Plants 🌿

🐒 Monkey

Monkeys are clever animals that can climb trees.

Food: Fruits 🍌

🐬 Dolphin

Dolphins are intelligent animals that live in the sea.

Food: Fish 🐟
